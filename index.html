The opportunity

Dubai loves saunas and yachts. Nobody offers both.

AED 200–250

per person per hour at sauna and ice-bath studios, in shared spaces. Sauna culture is growing.

For VIP guests

Hotel spas are public. High-profile guests want a space for their group alone.

Hull & machinery and third-party liability before launch. No alcohol before rounds; round time limits.
