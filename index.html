Dubai loves saunas and yachts. Nobody offers both.

Risks

The main risks, and what we do about each

1

Demand below 50% / 20%

Pre-sell 100 sessions and sign hotel LOIs before ordering the hull

2

Anchorage or towing not permitted

DMA pre-consultation before tranche 2; Plan B: operate moored at the berth

3

Summer demand drop

Cold plunge focus, early and late slots, haul-out in August

4

Wind over ~22 kn, waves over ~1.2 m

Booking freeze, early tow back, rebook policy

5

Guest health incident

Host on board, defibrillator, round limits, no alcohol before rounds, insurance

6

Build cost overrun

Fixed-price contract, 10% contingency, milestone payments
