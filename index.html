The idea

Dubai loves saunas and yachts. Nobody offers both in one private experience.

ON LAND

Sauna culture is growing

Sauna and ice-bath studios charge AED 200–250 per person per hour, in shared spaces.

AT SEA

Yachts lack wellness

Charters sell the sea and privacy, but offer no sauna, cold plunge or spa host.

FOR VIPs

Privacy is scarce

Hotel spas are public. High-profile guests want a space for their group alone.

How a day works

From sunrise tow
to marina lights.

06:30
06:30

Prepare

Safety checklist, pre-heat on shore power, linen and bar stocked.

07:00

Tow out

Our own workboat tows the platform at low speed to the approved anchorage off JBR. Anchor ball up.

08:30

Session one

The workboat brings the first group from the Marina pier.

11:30

Session two

The host turns the cabin around in 30 minutes. New group, fresh linen.

14:30

Session three

Afternoon light over the skyline. The workboat stays close all day.

17:15

Tow back

Last guests ashore, navigation lights on.

18:15

Service

Recharge, pump-out, fresh water, waste offloaded at the marina.

A host is on board whenever guests are aboard. If sea operation is not permitted, the platform runs moored at its berth.

Safety and permits

Licensed before the first guest.

On board

Host always present, 10 lifejackets, 2 lifebuoys, fire suppression, defibrillator, VHF radio, COLREGs lights and anchor ball.

Permits

Dubai Maritime Authority design review, vessel registration and licence; DMA permit for the day anchorage and towing; Civil Defence; berth contract.

Insurance

Hull & machinery and third-party liability in place before launch.

House rules

No alcohol before rounds. Round time limits.

Legal basis: Federal Decree-Law 43/2023; Dubai Law 11/2010 and its bylaw (Exec. Council Res. 11/2013); DMA Admin. Res. 5/2024 and 1/2025. To be confirmed by a UAE lawyer.
